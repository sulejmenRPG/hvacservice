Who We Are

About KD Scala Ltd

KD Scala Ltd is a London-based mechanical services company specialising in HVAC, heating, ventilation, and plumbing systems.

We work with both residential and commercial clients, providing practical, reliable solutions backed by real on-site experience. From plant room maintenance to emergency repairs, our focus is always on delivering safe, efficient, and long-lasting results.

What We Do

We support clients across a wide range of services, including:

  • Air conditioning installation, servicing, and repairs
  • Heating systems and boiler works
  • Ventilation systems and airflow management
  • General plumbing and mechanical services
  • Reactive maintenance and emergency call-outs

Our work ranges from small domestic jobs to complex commercial systems, always delivered with the same level of care and attention.
